Real recovery — ups and downs
What we imagine — a straight line
Ups and downs are part of the process — not against it.
A hard week does not erase the progress of the previous ones.
What matters is the trend over several weeks — not a single day's snapshot.
The goal: foundations,
not a quick fix.
We're not trying to fix everything in 6 months, but to build supports that last.
🌙 Sleep
Recover sleep that truly restores — the basis for everything else.
🧘 Stress regulation
Soothe the nervous system, a little, every day.
⚡ Energy
Rebuild your reserves gradually, without forcing.
🤝 Social connection
Lean on the right people around you.
🌱 These supports will keep serving you well after the program ends.
You are not a spectator.
Small, regular actions matter more than performance.
Your role
Move forward at your own pace — consistency over intensity.
Ask your questions and co-decide with your doctor, at every step.
What the program does
It supports you day to day.
It measures what matters week after week.
It flags the important signals to your doctor.
And what it does not do: it does not replace your medical follow-up or your treatment.
